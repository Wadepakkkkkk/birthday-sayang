A SECRET LETTER FOR YOU ♡

To my Sayang,

There’s something I’ve been wanting to say from the deepest part of my heart.

First of all, I’m sorry.

I’m sorry for the mistakes I’ve made, for the things I did wrong, and for the moments when my actions may have hurt you, disappointed you, or made you feel like you weren’t being loved the way you deserved to be.

I know that saying “I’m sorry” doesn’t automatically erase what happened. Words alone can’t undo the past, and I know a promise means nothing if my actions don’t follow it.

But I want you to know that I truly regret my mistakes.

If I could go back and change the moments where I hurt you, I would. Unfortunately, I can’t change what has already happened. The only thing I can do now is learn from it, become better, and make sure I never put you through the same thing again.

And that is something I promise you.

I promise I won’t repeat the same mistakes again.

Not because I’m afraid of losing you, but because I finally understand that when you truly care about someone, you have to protect their heart too.

I never want my actions to become the reason you question your worth, your place in my life, or how much you mean to me.

You mean more to me than I probably know how to explain.

Sometimes I may not express myself perfectly. Sometimes I might say the wrong thing, react the wrong way, or fail to show you what is actually inside my heart.

But please believe me when I say that having you in my life is something I never want to take for granted.

You’ve given me memories that I’ll always remember. You’ve made ordinary days feel special without even trying. There are conversations, laughs, random moments, and little things between us that probably seem insignificant to everyone else, but somehow mean so much to me.

And because you mean that much to me, I want to become better for you and for us.

I’m not going to promise that I’ll suddenly become a perfect person, because I know that wouldn’t be real.

But I can promise you effort.

I can promise you honesty.

I can promise you that I’ll listen more, understand more, think before I act, and be more careful with the heart of the person I love.

And most importantly, when I make a promise to you, I want my actions to be the proof.

I don’t expect you to forget everything just because I wrote this letter. I don’t expect a few beautiful words to fix every mistake.

I just hope that, little by little, I can show you that I mean everything I’m saying here.

Thank you for still being here.

Thank you for the patience you’ve given me, even during the times when I probably didn’t deserve it.

Thank you for every chance, every conversation, every laugh, every memory, and every piece of your heart that you’ve shared with me.

I’m sorry for every moment when I made things harder than they needed to be.

I’m sorry for the times I could have treated you better.

And I’m sorry for anything I did that made your heart hurt because of me.

If there is one thing I want you to remember from this letter, it’s this:

I learned from my mistakes.

I don’t want to repeat them.

I don’t want to go backwards.

I want to grow, to do better, and to love you in a way that makes you feel safe, appreciated, respected, and genuinely happy.

Maybe I can’t rewrite the chapters where I made mistakes.

But if you let me, I’d love to make the next chapters better.

More laughter.
More memories.
More understanding.
More honesty.
More love.

And fewer reasons for either of us to ever feel hurt.

Out of all the people I could have met, somehow life brought me to you.

And despite everything, if I had the chance to choose again…

I would still choose you. ♡

Not only in this universe.

Not only in another universe.

But in every version of my life where I get the chance to find you.

I’d still hope our paths cross.

I’d still want to know you.

I’d still want to make those memories with you.

And I’d still choose to love you.

So this is my little secret letter to you — not just an apology, but a promise.

I’m sorry for my mistakes.

I’ve learned from them.

I promise I won’t repeat them again.

And from now on, I’ll let my actions prove the words I wrote here.

You deserve the better version of me.

And I’m going to do my best to become that person.

I love you, Sayang.
More than these words could ever explain. ♡

Always yours,

Bubu ♡
